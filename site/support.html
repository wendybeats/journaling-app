Common questions

I wrote something I want to change.

Endpaper can't edit or delete committed writing — that's the product, not a missing feature. What you wrote is what you wrote. If this doesn't suit how you journal, the export (below) gives you everything in plain text, and Endpaper may simply not be your notebook.

Is my writing backed up?

If Back up with iCloud is on in Settings, your notebook syncs to your private iCloud and follows your Apple ID to a new phone. If it's off, your writing exists only on your device — deleting the app deletes the notebook. You can check which mode you're in at Settings, on the line under the toggle.

How do I get my writing out?

Settings → Export your notebook. One Markdown file, every day you've written, yours forever. No lock-in, ever.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Endpaper → Cancel. Your notebook stays on your device either way — if you resubscribe later, writing resumes exactly where it stopped, and reading your notebook is never locked.

I have a founding-member code.

In the app: Settings → Redeem a code. Your free year starts the moment it's accepted.

Where's the Android / web version?

There isn't one yet. One person, one platform, done properly first.

Privacy

The short version: your writing never leaves your device except to your own private iCloud, and there are no analytics of any kind. The long version is the privacy policy — it's short too, because there's nothing to disclose.
