# Founder scripts — the desk session

*2026-08-20 · One recording session, three pieces. IG Reels + TikTok
(same cuts; TikTok tolerates the rawer take). Tone mellow — intensity
lives in the claim, never the delivery. One claim per video.*

Setup for all three: Wendell at a desk, his real filled journals stacked
in front of him. Single locked shot, natural light, no jump-cut energy.
Catalog audio low or none. B-roll inserts are screen recordings from
build 11 (the weekly deck is the demo).

---

## Piece 1 — "I never read them back" (FLAGSHIP, record first)

Hook family: confession / relatable failure. The claim carries the
intensity; delivery stays flat.

**Runtime target:** 35–45s

| Beat | Time | Spoken (his voice, loose is fine) | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–4s | "I've filled every one of these journals. And I couldn't tell you what's written in a single one of them." | Hands on the stack, face off-camera or half-frame. Journals are the star. |
| Problem | 4–12s | "That's the secret about journaling nobody says out loud. You write to understand yourself — and then you never read it again. All of that thinking, shelved." | Flips one open, closes it. |
| Turn | 12–20s | "So I built Endpaper. One journal, on your phone. It still feels permanent — when the day ends, the page seals, and you can't touch it. No AI. Locked behind Face ID. It's yours." | Phone enters frame on top of the stack. |
| The claim | 20–32s | "But here's the real reason I built it: it's the only journal that reads you back. Every week it hands you your own words — what kept surfacing, the question you asked yourself, the word you kept using." | CUT to screen recording: weekly deck beats (thread → question → the turn). |
| Close | 32–40s | "Even if you never re-read a page — this journal does it for you. The writing was never the point. The reading back is." | Back to the desk. Hold a beat. |

Caption:
```
I filled all of these journals and never read one of them back.
```
First comment: standard ("I wrote this in the Endpaper app" does NOT
apply here — founder pieces get: "Endpaper is on the App Store — link in
bio.")

**Overlay text:** minimal — one line max at the hook ("every journal
I've ever filled") and the claim ("the only journal that reads you
back").

## Piece 2 — "You can't edit it. On purpose." (permanence/privacy)

Hook family: deliberate wrongness — a feature that sounds like a bug.

**Runtime target:** 25–30s

- Hook (0–4s): "I made a journal you can't edit. On purpose."
- Body (4–16s): "When the day ends, the page seals. No fixing
  yesterday's spelling, no rewriting who you were. That's what paper
  got right, and every notes app gets wrong."
- Privacy stack (16–24s): "No AI reading it. No account. Face ID and
  your phone — that's the whole system."
- Close (24–28s): "Permanent like paper. Private like paper. Just never
  lost like paper." (taps the journal stack)

Caption:
```
A journal you can't edit. On purpose.
```

---

## Piece 3 — "If you're in therapy…" (use-case, niche reach)

Hook family: direct address to a named audience (therapy / new
relationship / self-understanding). Soft claims only — no clinical
promises.

**Runtime target:** 20–25s

- Hook (0–3s): "If you're in therapy, this is for you."
- Body (3–14s): "The hardest question in the room is 'what's been coming
  up this week?' — and you lived it, so you can't see it. Endpaper can.
  It hands you your week in your own words: what kept surfacing, the
  question you kept circling."
- Close (14–20s): "Walk in already knowing what to talk about." + screen
  recording of the weekly thread beat.

Caption:
```
Know what to bring to your next session.
```
(Variant hooks for later re-cuts: "new relationship" / "trying to
understand yourself" — same body, swap the address.)
