# Endpaper — Growth Arcs

**Date:** August 12, 2026 · The three-arc marketing strategy, with the
gates that graduate one arc into the next. Companion to
docs/endpaper-marketing-brief.md (positioning) and
docs/endpaper-content-engine.md (the machine that makes the assets).
Live operational state (cadence, hook bank, results log) stays in
tools/content/hooks.md.

Arcs are gated by metrics, not by calendar. Each arc keeps running
underneath the next one — Arc 2 doesn't stop the quote engine, it
demotes it to filler; Arc 3 doesn't stop feature demos, it aims them at
niches.

---

## Arc 1 — The lifestyle (now)

Value-led reach while the account is cold. Not value-*only*: the
standing promo ratio (≤1 in 4 posts) keeps a soft sell in the stream —
the app is live and founding-thirty exists; walling off the sell would
waste the reach we're buying with the value posts.

1. **Quotes** — the infinite cheap engine. Crowded space, but the posts
   travel (first data: Reels at 9× stills, 98% non-follower reach) and
   the typing format keeps the product in frame. Bench refreshed by the
   weekly Eden mining pass (outlier-ranked quote posts) + Wendell's
   finds. Watch the honest metric: **profile taps and site clicks per
   Reel**, not views — quote reach is unqualified by default; the tap
   is what qualifies it.
2. **Dot-matrix motion** — the moat format. Quotes are borrowed
   content; moving dots that tell a story are content only Endpaper can
   make. Ongoing campaign spine: *write consistently, be more mindful,
   get your words given back to you*. First piece: "what's really going
   on inside your mind" (chaos of drifting dots → settles into the
   month grid as the writing lands). Template lives beside the quote
   renderer; same pipeline, same registers.
3. **Founder voice** — one-take videos (the format IS the product
   rule: recorded once, mistakes kept), philosophy of writing, cross-
   posted with Wendell's personal brand for borrowed trust.
4. **Channels**: Instagram + Threads. 2×/day cadence via the daily kit
   routine.

**Gate to Arc 2 — all three:**
- App approved and stable on the App Store (no review-blocking bugs
  two consecutive weeks). ✅ *Approved & live 2026-08-12 —
  apps.apple.com/app/id6795154721; stability clock starts now.*
- Reels drive ≥25 endpaper.space clicks/week (attribution links
  /ig, /th) for two consecutive weeks.
- Founding-thirty offer fully claimed, or 30 days post-launch —
  whichever comes first (the seed cohort is Arc 2's UGC well).

## Arc 2 — The sell

The app is launched, tested, and good. Show it.

1. **Product-use content** — voice capture, the living type shrink,
   photographing a paper page, calendar choreography, day-boundary
   editing. Real captures first (the content engine renders these
   pixel-perfectly); **AI-generated footage is allowed for
   demonstration b-roll** — scenario/lifestyle shots of the product in
   hands and lives we can't film (the Coconote pattern), under the
   guardrails below.
2. **Founder voice, aimed** — why I built it, what it costs to say
   nothing, permanence as a feature.
3. **Quotes demoted to filler** — the engine keeps running, fills the
   gaps between product pieces.
4. **Real-user material** — founding-thirty asked for *sentences*, not
   videos (text testimonials over screenshots of their own pages, with
   permission). A single real sentence outperforms a synthetic rave.
5. **Channels**: + TikTok (the quote-motion and dot formats port for
   free). First paid tests: small budgets, against the click metric we
   already trust.

**AI-footage guardrails (the line we don't cross):**
- AI may **demonstrate**, never **testify**. No synthetic person
  presented as a user; no invented "results"; no fake screen
  recordings of writing that was never written.
- Product UI in AI footage must match the real product (composite the
  real captures in, don't let a model hallucinate our interface).
- Voice: if a piece is AI-assembled and a reasonable viewer would
  assume it's real documentary footage, it isn't posted. The brand is
  honesty; the audience we're attracting screenshots hypocrisy.
- The no-AI promise is about the **product** (no AI reads your
  journal). Marketing production tooling is a different thing — but we
  never blur the two in copy.

**Gate to Arc 3 — all three:**
- Paid conversion known: trial→paid ≥ 8% or an ads CAC that pencils
  against $[price]/yr (kill ads otherwise, keep organic).
- D30 retention of new users ≥ 20% (people who stay are the proof the
  niches deserve investment).
- 1,000 true followers across channels (not vanity — the retargeting
  seed and the newsletter feed).

## Arc 3 — The scale

1. **Niche use-cases as content, not product forks.** Endpaper is the
   blank canvas; we show what it's for:
   - *Alongside therapy* — the prompts your therapist gives you land on
     a page that keeps you honest, and Reflections gives your own words
     back at week's end. (Language guardrail: we support the work, we
     never do the work — no "CBT companion," no clinical claims, no
     diagnosis-adjacent copy. "It gives you your words back," never
     "it gives you insights.")
   - *Relationships in ink* — what you wrote about them in March, kept
     exactly as March wrote it.
   - *Dreams, recorded* — voice capture before the dream fades. The
     record, not the interpretation.
   - Each niche gets a pinned story album, a prompt list, and a
     founder piece. Product changes only where a niche reveals a
     universal gap (e.g., an import path), never a fork.
2. **Founder voice** → mental health and well-being, the case for
   writing as maintenance.
3. **Long-form**: YouTube essays; webinars/meetups only if the
   audience asks for them by name (heavy for a solo operation —
   earn it first).
4. **Ads in full swing** — positioned against the empty quadrant
   (well-designed AND gives your words back; the competition is
   neither). Prerequisite product ticket: **an import path** (Day One
   switchers can't arrive with ten years of entries stranded).

## The owned channel (starts in Arc 1, compounds forever)

**One letter a month** from endpaper.space. Quiet, on-voice: one idea
about writing, one user line (with permission), one product note.
Signup on the site footer + link-in-bio. Rented reach feeds it; nobody
can algorithm it away. Start now — the first hundred addresses are
worth more than the next thousand followers.

## Standing rules (all arcs)

- Promo ratio ≤ 1 in 4. Voice: quiet, complete sentences, no
  exclamation marks, no hashtag soup.
- No fake attributions on quotes; unattributed beats misattributed.
- Never a fabricated testimonial, in any arc, in any format.
- Friday pass: kill/scale on the week's data; mine quotes; log
  everything in hooks.md.
