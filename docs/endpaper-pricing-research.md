## Analysis

Three bands: utility sync-unlocks at **$18–24**, mid-premium at
**$36–50** (Stoic, Daylio, Five Minute Journal, Day One, Journey), and
AI/wellness-premium at **$60–150**. **$29.99 sits in the gap** — exactly
Bear's price, i.e. priced like a freemium sync unlock while asking for a
Day One-grade commitment.

The hard-paywall products that exist (Reflectly in practice, Waking Up,
iA Writer's pay-up-front) all sit **at or above** the premium band —
hard paywalls sustain higher prices, not lower; when the paywall carries
all revenue, underpricing caps LTV without buying conversion.

Nearly every comp offers a monthly at 2.5–3× the annual
monthly-equivalent (a steering price). Annual-only is cleaner and
matches the committed-practice positioning, but raises the trial-end
hurdle — a year-sized decision after seven days.

## Recommendation

Launch at **$34.99–39.99/yr** — beside Stoic and Five Minute Journal,
clearly under Day One — keeping the 7-day trial, with intro-offer
headroom (e.g. $29.99 first year) instead of a permanently low sticker.
**Annual-only at launch**; hold a ~$4.99–5.99 monthly in reserve as the
first experiment if trial→paid conversion underperforms. If $29.99 is
kept, treat it as a deliberate penetration price with a planned raise,
not the anchor.

**Decision: Wendell's, at the D4 pricing pass** (the product isn't
created until then; nothing blocks on this today).
