# Endpaper — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE — set at publication]
**Applies to:** the Endpaper iOS app and endpaper.space

## The short version

We don't collect your data. Not "we collect it carefully" — we never
receive it in the first place. Endpaper has no accounts, no analytics, no
tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere in the product.

## Your writing

Everything you write in Endpaper is stored on your device. If you turn on
**Back up with iCloud**, your writing syncs to *your* private iCloud
database — storage that belongs to your Apple account, governed by your
Apple ID and Apple's terms. We have no access to it: no admin panel, no
database, no copy. There is nothing on our side to breach, subpoena, or
sell.

If you leave iCloud backup off, your writing exists only on your device.

## Reflections

Endpaper's reflections (weekly, monthly, yearly) are computed **on your
device**. Your writing is not sent anywhere to produce them.

If a future version offers cloud-assisted reflections, that feature will
be strictly opt-in, clearly explained at the moment you enable it, and
this policy will be updated first. It will never be on by default.

## What we don't do

- No account, profile, or sign-up — we don't know your name or email.
- No analytics or usage tracking of any kind, first- or third-party.
- No third-party SDKs. The app contains our code and Apple's frameworks,
  nothing else.
- No advertising, no data sharing, no data sale — there is no data.

## Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a **local notification**, scheduled and
delivered entirely on your device. No push servers are involved.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see
your payment details. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by
Apple's own privacy policy.

## Export and deletion

Your writing is yours. You can export your entire notebook as plain text
at any time (Settings → Export). Deleting the app removes the local copy;
your iCloud copy is under your control in iOS Settings → iCloud. We hold
nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete.

## Children

Endpaper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from anyone — child or adult.

## The website

endpaper.space is a static page. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be visible here with a new
date, and any change that touches your writing will be announced in the
app before it takes effect.
