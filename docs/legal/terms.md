## 3. Permanence — read this one

Endpaper is built on a deliberate rule: **what you write stays written.**
The app has no edit and no delete for committed entries, the way a pen
has no undo. This is a feature, stated up front during onboarding — not a
malfunction. Think before you commit a page; the point is to commit.

You always control the whole notebook: you can export everything as plain
text, and deleting the app (and your iCloud data, in iOS Settings)
removes your copy entirely.
