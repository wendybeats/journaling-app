## TestFlight "What to Test" (per-build notes; 4000 chars max)

> First build of Endpaper — a journal reduced to its essence.
>
> Please live with it for real: one honest note a day, on your actual
> life, for at least a week. The product only shows itself with real use.
>
> Worth your attention:
> • Does opening the app → writing feel instant?
> • The permanence rule: entries can't be edited or deleted, on purpose.
>   Does the app teach you that clearly before your first surprise?
> • After ~a week: the weekly reflection (say yes to the card). Does it
>   feel like YOUR week, honestly mirrored — or like a horoscope?
> • The calendar: tap a month, tap a day. Watch the dots move.
> • Anything that feels like an app instead of a notebook — banners,
>   spinners, blue links — is a bug in this product. Report it.
>
> Known: text size doesn't yet follow the system's Dynamic Type;
> the trial is simulated until the App Store product goes live.

## Welcome message (send with the invite — iMessage/email register)

> I built a journal. It's called Endpaper.
>
> It does almost nothing on purpose: you open it, today's page is
> waiting, you write, you close it. What you write can't be edited or
> deleted — like ink. Each day you write, a dot fills in. After a week
> it can read your own words back to you, if you ask it to.
>
> Nothing you write leaves your phone (and your iCloud, if you turn
> that on). No account, no analytics. I literally cannot see your
> journal — architecture, not a promise.
>
> The TestFlight link is below. Two asks: use it for real for a week —
> one honest note a day — and then tell me the truth, especially where
> it annoyed you. The trial screen is fake-free for beta; you'll never
> be charged.
>
> — W

## Feedback questions (send after ~a week)

1. How many days did you actually write? (Honest number — the dots know.)
2. Did permanence change what you wrote? Better or worse?
3. The weekly reflection: did it feel like you? Anything that felt off,
   reachy, or wrong?
4. What almost made you stop opening it?
5. Would you pay $39.99/year at the end of the trial? If not — what's
   the number, or what's missing?

## Beta logistics

- Internal group "Core": Wendell's devices.
- External group "Friends": ≤20 testers, invite by email or public link.
- Feedback channel: TestFlight's built-in feedback (screenshots attach
  automatically) + hello@endpaper.space for longer thoughts.
- Cadence: fresh build as fixes land; note headline changes in "What to
  Test" each time.
- Grandfathering (decided by default in the launch plan): beta testers
  get a 1-year promo code at public launch as thanks.
